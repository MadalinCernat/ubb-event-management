# User research: interviews

Participants: S1, S2, and S3 are students; S3 also organizes events for a student organization.

## 1. How do you currently discover or organize student events, and what is the biggest difficulty?

- **S1:** I find events on Instagram stories and WhatsApp groups, often too late. There is no single place.
- **S2:** I hear about events from friends and faculty Facebook groups. I miss most of the others.
- **S3:** I post on Instagram and a WhatsApp group and collect sign-ups in a Google Form. The hardest part is counting sign-ups and stopping at capacity by hand.

## 2. What information or functionality do you need before deciding to register for an event?

- **S1:** The date, place, topic, and whether there are spots left. I also want to know if it is free.
- **S2:** The topic, language, and time. I would like a reminder before the event.
- **S3:** Students keep asking me the place, the time, and whether there are spots left, so that should be visible. I also want to see who registered.

## 3. What would make you use, or avoid using, a dedicated event-management platform?

- **S1:** I would use it if sign-up is quick and the list is easy to search. I would avoid it if it needs a long form.
- **S2:** I would not create another account unless registering takes seconds, and I do not want to be forced to use a UBB email.
- **S3:** I would use it if creating an event is quicker than my form and posts. I would avoid it if students do not use it.

## Conclusion: what we changed

- Event list and detail pages are public; sign-in is needed only to register.
- The list and detail pages show date, place, and remaining spots.
- Registration is one action with no extra form fields.
- Any email is accepted at sign-up; a UBB email is not required.
- Added: managers can see the list of registered users for their events.
- Kept out of the MVP: reminders, messaging, and attendance.
